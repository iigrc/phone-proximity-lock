PHONE PROXIMITY LOCK 0.2.1

Requirements
macOS 13 or later. The app includes Apple silicon and Intel executables.
Bluetooth must be enabled on the Mac and selected device.
Tested on one Apple silicon Mac. Intel execution and other macOS versions
have not been tested here; pilot with one colleague before broader use.

Install and choose a device
1. Unzip this package and move Phone Proximity Lock.app to Applications.
2. Open the app. It lives in the menu bar as Phone Lock.
3. In Settings & devices, choose a device from the paired-device list and
   click Use selected device. This starts automatic locking.
4. If the device is not paired yet, use Pair a new device. Make the phone
   discoverable in its Bluetooth settings and confirm matching codes on
   both devices. You can also pair in macOS Bluetooth settings and click
   Refresh in Phone Lock.
5. Optionally enable Start Phone Lock when I log in.

Automatic unlocking is OPTIONAL and OFF on a fresh installation.
The app can be used for locking only without saving a password.
To enable unlocking, select its checkbox in Settings & devices. Grant
Accessibility access (called Device Control and Data Access on newer
macOS versions), then enter your own Mac login password in the secure
local prompt. The password is checked locally and stored in your login
Keychain; it is not sent anywhere or written to a settings file.
The setup offers a lock/unlock test. Leave the keyboard and Touch ID
alone for that test, then unlock manually if needed.

Defaults
- Lock after 20 seconds of disconnection.
- If enabled, unlock after a stable 1-second reconnection.
- Only locks made by this app can be automatically unlocked.
- Manual locks, first login after restart, and logins after logging out
  stay manual.
- Choosing a DIFFERENT device switches automatic unlocking off again.
- Normal system sleep is unchanged. The app does not keep the Mac awake.
  Detection resumes once the Mac wakes; it cannot poll Bluetooth while
  the Mac is asleep.

Bluetooth range varies and can reach beyond the room. Devices that do
not maintain a connection, including some watches, are unsuitable as a
lock trigger. Test your device's disconnect/reconnect behavior first.
The app uses a fresh, authenticated Bluetooth worker for each connection
and restarts a stalled worker rather than waiting indefinitely for a
callback from the previous connection.

Pause or change settings
Use Phone Lock in the menu bar, or reopen the app to show Settings &
devices. You can pause locking/automation, pause automatic unlocking,
change delays, select another device, or remove the saved unlock password.
Your settings and bounded activity log live in your own account under
~/Library/Application Support/Phone Proximity Lock/.
Each Mac has its own configuration and Keychain entry. The distributed
package contains no selected device, account settings, logs, or passwords.

Opening this local prototype
This package is locally signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized.
macOS or company device-management policy may block its first launch.
Use your organization's approval process. Apple describes the relevant
per-app Open Anyway flow here:
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445
Do not disable Gatekeeper globally. For a normal company-wide release,
arrange Developer ID signing and Apple notarization with your developer/IT
team. Permission grants may need refreshing after a locally signed update.

Upgrading the original local version
Quit the existing Phone Lock before opening this version. It can import
the original account's device and delay settings when present. Automatic
unlocking is paused on migration so the new build can be authorized.
The existing saved password stays in that account's Keychain.

Uninstall
In the app, turn off Start Phone Lock when I log in and optionally remove
the saved unlock password. Quit Phone Lock, then move the app to Trash.
You may also remove its own Application Support folder if no longer needed.
